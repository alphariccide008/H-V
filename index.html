About HydroVanta

Technology Needs Control.

Founded in 1980, HydroVanta has grown into a globally recognized manufacturer of fluid control components. With decades of engineering expertise, we supply electric pumps, solenoid valves, vibration pumps, pressure switches, and flow switches to industries across six continents.

Our product portfolio is engineered to handle all types of fluids — including water, gas, steam, and air — with the precision and reliability that modern industry demands. HydroVanta is trusted by OEMs, system integrators, and industrial operators worldwide.

  • ✓ Engineering Excellence — decades of R&D backed by real-world application data.
  • ✓ Global Reach — strong distribution networks in Europe, the Americas, Asia and beyond.
  • ✓ Industry Standards — all products comply with international safety and quality certifications.
  • ✓ Custom Solutions — bespoke configurations available for specialist applications.
Our Products

Complete Fluid Control Portfolio

Every component we manufacture is built to the same standard: precision-engineered, field-proven, and ready for the demands of real industrial environments.

⚡
Electric Pumps

High-performance centrifugal and displacement pumps designed for continuous duty in commercial and industrial fluid systems. Available in a wide range of flow rates and pressure specifications.

🔧
Solenoid Valves

Electromechanically operated valves for precise, reliable control of water, gas, steam, oil and air flows. Available in 2-way and 3-way configurations for direct or pilot operation.

〰️
Vibration Pumps

Electromagnetically driven vibration pumps offering quiet, efficient operation for low-flow, high-pressure applications — ideal for coffee machines, water heaters, and small appliances.

📊
Pressure Switches

Electro-mechanical and electronic pressure switches for automatic system activation, deactivation and protection. Precise set-point adjustment with wide operating pressure ranges.

💧
Flow Switches

Flow detection and monitoring devices that enable automated system responses to changes in fluid flow. Suitable for heating, cooling, irrigation and industrial process lines.

🏭
Custom Engineering

Have a unique fluid control challenge? Our engineering team designs bespoke components and sub-assemblies tailored to your exact application requirements and operating conditions.
